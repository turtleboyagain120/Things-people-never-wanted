TIPS & TRICKS

READ THIS FIRST

FAULTLINE is a controller-first 2D momentum shooter. Keep moving, use shields, and chain slide jumps into air control.

Win The Run

Move fast, collect drops, survive boss prompts, and use checkpoints. Falling costs health and returns you to safety when possible.

Controller Basics

Left stick moves. Right stick aims. A jumps. LT slides. RT fires. Y reloads. Start opens pause.

Extra Controls

LB dashes, RB fires shotgun, X starts assisted combat, B cycles weapons. D-pad moves menus. A selects highlighted menu items.

Half Mantle Cover

Clip a top edge to hold cover. Your body stays protected while your head and gun peek out. Press A to vault or LT/back away to drop.

Air Strafe

Tap LT, release it, then press A within the short window. Holding LT only gives precision during that active air-strafe.

Air Turning

While airborne, flick the left stick left or right to correct your path. It gives a small boost for sharp turns.

Style Meter

Slide, strafe, wall jump, reload cancel, weak spot, and kill chains raise style. Faster kills score harder.

Risk Lanes

Many sections have a safer lower line and a faster risky upper route. The risky route gives ammo, shields, or rescue gear.

Rescue Grapple

Rare yellow hooks save one missed fall by pulling you back toward the nearest safe platform.

No Ammo Plan

If every gun is empty, RT automatically becomes a fast assisted punch or kick. You are never helpless.

Weapons

AR is steady, SMG sprays fast, pistol hits hard and slow, shotgun is your close-range emergency button.

Weak Spots

Head and core shots stagger enemies and can pay back ammo or shield. Melee finishers work best on staggered targets.

Enemy Roles

Suppressors pin you, rushers close distance, and guards hold platforms. Bosses can copy habits you use too much.

Drops

Kills can drop ammo, hearts, shields, coins, and weapons. Shields restore armor and should be grabbed before hard fights.

Settings

Smoothing changes the motion-blur look. Tactic AI tunes enemy prediction. Stick Response changes controller curve.

Boss Copy

If you repeat one tactic too much, bosses may copy it. The screen flashes BOSS COPY, then the boss shifts movement for a short burst.

Theme Landmarks

The run focuses on polished rooftops: skyline layers, distant elevated trains, cranes, skyways, radio towers, vents, and water tanks.

Auto Inventory

Turn it on if gun swapping feels busy. It can help reload, pick weapons, save ammo, and choose pickups smarter.

Boss Prompts

When a prompt appears, press the shown button or stick direction before the bar drains. Some bosses ask for repeated taps.

Rooftop Stream

The level machine mixes, mutates, and checks rooftop tools every run. It avoids repeats and rejects cluttered layouts.

Power Cubes

Hit the glowing cube to trigger overdrive. You grow temporarily, hit harder, and refill momentum for big fights.

Troubleshooting

No controller? connect it and press a button. Movement too twitchy? use Stick Response Expo or Movement Assist. Too intense? turn Comfort FX on.

If You Get Stuck

Use Start, choose New Run, or fall to respawn at a checkpoint. If ammo is gone, close distance and use RT melee.

If Enemies Hurt

Stop standing still. Jump, strafe, dash, and collect shields. Enemy bullets remove armor first, then hearts.
