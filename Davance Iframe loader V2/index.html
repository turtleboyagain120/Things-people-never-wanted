📖 Everything You Need to Know

1. What is an Iframe?

An iframe is like a magic window inside your webpage. It lets you show another whole website right here on the page — like putting a TV screen on your wall that plays a different channel from the internet!

2. How the Loading Paths Work

  • Direct Load: Opens the site exactly as typed.
  • Path options: Add extra words to reach different pages.
  • Custom UTM: Adds fully customizable marketing params (source, medium, campaign, term, content).
  • Smart Path Detection: Automatically tries common embed paths until one works.
  • Custom Path: Specify your own path for specialized loading.

3. Custom Headers & Encryption

Headers (X-Tracking-ID, X-Source, X-Campaign) are injected invisibly—not in the URL. Perfect for ABM privacy. Enable encryption to hash sensitive parameters so they can't be tampered with.

4. Dynamic Resize & Aspect Ratio

Auto-resize keeps your iframe perfectly sized to the content. Lock a 16:9 ratio with min/max constraints to prevent layout shifts that hurt conversion metrics.

5. Analytics Event Queue

Events are buffered if the network is slow and retried with exponential backoff. UTM data is captured even on flaky connections—not fire-and-forget. Flush manually or let the queue auto-send.

6. Quick Tips

  • HTTPS is added automatically.
  • Try different paths if a site doesn't load.
  • Use Reset to start over.
  • The status bar shows queued events.
  • Save/Load settings to keep your config.
  • Smart Path Detection attempts common embed paths automatically.
  • Create custom presets for your favorite configurations.
